Frequently asked questions

Where do I get channels or films?

vioKoni doesn't provide any content and doesn't recommend providers. It plays sources you already have: an Xtream Codes account or an M3U playlist from a provider you are legally entitled to use.

Which sources work?

Xtream Codes accounts (live TV, films, series, TV guide) and M3U playlists (live TV, with an XMLTV guide if your playlist names one). Several sources can be active at the same time.

Why do artwork and descriptions only appear with a TMDB key?

Artwork, logos and descriptions come from The Movie Database. TMDB gives free API keys for personal use; add yours under Settings → Services → TMDB. Without a key, vioKoni shows what your provider supplies.

How do I move my library to another Apple TV?

Open Settings → Manage in Browser, pair your computer or phone with the code on the TV, and download a backup. Set a backup password if credentials and keys should be included. Import it the same way on the other Apple TV.

How do I lock adult categories?

Set a PIN under Settings → Categories & PIN. Adult categories are then locked automatically, and you can hide or lock any other category.

How do I start over?

Settings → About → Reset App deletes all sources, credentials and your library from this Apple TV.

Does vioKoni collect my data?

No. See the privacy policy.
