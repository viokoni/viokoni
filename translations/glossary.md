# Glossary

Fixed terms per language. Use them everywhere, so the same thing has the same name on every screen.
vioKoni plays live TV, films and series from the user's own subscription (an Xtream Codes account or an M3U playlist) on Apple TV, iPhone and iPad.

## All languages

- **Never translate:** vioKoni, TMDB, Simkl, MDBList, IMDb, Rotten Tomatoes, Metacritic, VLC, AetherEngine, AVPlayer, Xtream Codes, M3U, XMLTV, EPG, HLS, MPEG-TS, HEVC, HDR, Dolby Vision, iCloud, Apple TV, Top Shelf, User-Agent, PIN.
- **Never add** "IPTV", provider names or channel names, and nothing that sounds like the app supplies content ("free channels", "watch everything"). vioKoni brings no content of its own.
- **Remote buttons** are named as Apple names them in your language (English: *Select*, *Menu*, *Play/Pause*).
- **Keep it short.** On Apple TV a button, a pill or a column has little room; aim for no more than about 30 % longer than the English.
- **Placeholders** (`%@`, `%lld`, `%1$@`) stay; reorder only with numbers (`%2$@ … %1$@`).
