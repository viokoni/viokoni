Age rating

vioKoni is rated 18+ on the App Store.

Why 18+

vioKoni ships with no channels, films, playlists or other content. It plays only the sources a user adds: an Xtream Codes account or an M3U playlist from a provider they are entitled to use.

We can't know or control what those sources contain. A provider's line-up may include films, series and live TV for any age, including categories meant for adults. Because such content can be played through the app, we rate it 18+.

Parental controls

  • Set a PIN under Settings → Categories & PIN.
  • Once a PIN is set, categories recognised as adult are locked automatically. Their titles also stay out of search, Home and the watchlist.
  • Any other category can be hidden, or locked with the PIN, one by one.
  • The category settings, the list of sources and resetting the app all ask for the PIN.

What vioKoni does not have

  • No content of its own, no content recommendations from us, no store for content.
  • No web browser, no chat, no user profiles visible to others, no advertising.
