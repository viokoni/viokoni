Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026 · Deutsch

vioKoni is a media player for Apple TV. It plays live TV and on-demand video from sources that you add yourself. This policy explains what happens to your data. The short version: we do not collect any data. vioKoni has no server of its own, no account with us, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Who is responsible

The person responsible and the postal address are given in the Impressum (legal notice). E-mail: support@viokoni.com

Data that stays on your Apple TV

Everything vioKoni stores is kept on your device:

  • Sources and credentials. Xtream Codes accounts and M3U playlist addresses you add. Passwords, playlist addresses and API keys are stored in the system keychain, never in plain text, and they are not synchronised to other devices.
  • Your library. Watch progress, watch history, watchlist, favourite channels, hidden categories and channels, your TMDB matches, and settings.
  • Caches. Channel lists, catalogs, TV guide data, artwork and metadata, so the app starts faster. tvOS may delete caches at any time.
  • Top Shelf. A small list of titles and pictures for the Apple TV Top Shelf, shared only with vioKoni's own Top Shelf extension on the same device.

We cannot see any of this. Deleting the app deletes it. Settings → About → Reset App deletes it as well.

Data vioKoni sends, and to whom

vioKoni only connects to services that you set up. Each request goes directly from your Apple TV to that service; nothing passes through us.

These services process data under their own privacy policies. Your content provider is chosen by you and is not related to vioKoni.

Manage in Browser

While Settings → Manage in Browser is open, your Apple TV runs a small web server that is reachable only inside your local network, and only from a browser that you pair with a code shown on the TV. It lets you manage sources, categories and backups from a computer or phone. Nothing is reachable from the internet. Leaving that page stops the server.

Backups

A backup file is created only when you ask for one in Manage in Browser, and it is saved where your browser saves downloads. Credentials and keys are included only if you set a backup password; they are then encrypted with it.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages and served through Cloudflare. It uses no cookies, no analytics and no embedded third-party content. When you open a page, the hosting providers process your IP address and the usual request data to deliver the page and protect against abuse. If you write to us by e-mail, we use your message only to answer it. If you open an issue on GitHub, GitHub's privacy policy applies, and your issue is public.

Children

vioKoni is not directed at children. Content comes from the source you add. Settings → Categories & PIN lets you hide categories or lock them with a PIN.

Your rights

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to show, correct or delete. For data held by your content provider or an optional service, please contact that provider. You may lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.
